v1.6.15 (Build 30)

What's New
- Improved passwordless sign-in so the remembered primary device can sign in again directly after being logged out.
- Added clearer fallback guidance when another device tries passwordless sign-in while the primary device is currently signed out.
- Added a Start again action on the passwordless waiting screen after expiry or other failed approval states.
- Updated passwordless approval prompts and sync-unlock dialogs to behave more reliably on larger screens and tablets.
- Refined account lookup messaging so unknown usernames now show a clearer account not available response.
- Continued stability fixes around passwordless approval, primary-device handling, and sync unlock recovery flows.
