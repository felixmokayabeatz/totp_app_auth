v1.13.0 (Build 53)

What's New
- Export all your accounts at once. Settings now has "Export All Accounts", in two forms: an encrypted backup protected by a passphrase you choose, or plain otpauth links that any other authenticator app can read. Until now the only way out was scanning one QR code per account.
- The plain export tells you plainly what it is before it writes anything: your 2FA secrets in readable text, to be moved where you need them and then deleted.
- Exporting reads the accounts on your device, so it works even if cloud sync is having problems.

Security
- Your synced vault can no longer be overwritten by a device working from an older copy. The server now checks that an upload is actually newer than what it holds and refuses it otherwise. Your app notices, re-reads the newer copy, merges, and carries on.
- Restoring a snapshot no longer replaces what is in the cloud. It reads and merges the stored copy first, keeps your record of deleted accounts instead of clearing it, and refuses if this device cannot read what is up there.

Fixes and Performance
- Faster notifications, device sessions and sign-in request lists, especially as they grow. The database can now find and order your rows directly instead of sorting them each time.
