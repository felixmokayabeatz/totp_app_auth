v1.12.0 (Build 52)

Security
- Your synced accounts are encrypted properly now. The old format could be unlocked by anyone who obtained the stored file, without needing your password at all, because of a weakness in how it was scrambled. It is replaced with an authenticated cipher, and your vault is converted the first time this version syncs. Nothing to do.
- Tampering with your stored vault is now detected instead of silently changing what comes back.
- Your password is put through 210,000 rounds of PBKDF2 before it unlocks anything. Guessing passwords against a stolen file used to be effectively free; now every guess costs an attacker the same work it costs your device. This is why unlocking takes a moment.
- Accounts on your device are no longer protected by a key that can be worked out from your email address. A random secret created on each install goes into it, so holding the file is no longer enough to read it.
- The app now refuses to read vault data in the old unauthenticated format except while converting it. That closes a downgrade where stripping four characters would have skipped the tamper check.

What's New
- Slow operations tell you what they are doing. Saving your recovery codes PDF, unlocking sync, repairing sync and checking a recovery code all show a spinner and a description instead of appearing to do nothing.

Fixes and Performance
- Existing recovery codes and keyrings keep working. Anything created under the older, weaker settings is upgraded automatically the first time you enter your password.
