v1.14.0 (Build 54)

Security
- Your vault is now encrypted with AES-256-GCM, the standard the rest of the industry uses, from a reviewed library. The previous method was built in-house. It was not broken, but "not broken as far as we can tell" is not the right standard for the app holding your second factor. Your data is converted as it is written, and anything still in the old format is read normally until then.
- Your sign-in tokens and vault key now live in your device's own secure store: Windows DPAPI, the Apple Keychain, the Android Keystore, or your Linux keyring. They used to sit in app storage behind a key that could be worked out from your computer's name and username. They are moved across automatically the first time they are read.
- On a system with no such store available, BeatzLock keeps using the previous method rather than refusing to sign you in.
- Signing out now stops push notifications properly. Alerts kept arriving and being handled for an account that had already signed out.

Fixes and Performance
- Tighter checks are now enforced across the whole codebase, covering the mistakes that caused real problems here: screens using stale references after an awaited call, listeners never cancelled, and errors quietly swallowed.
