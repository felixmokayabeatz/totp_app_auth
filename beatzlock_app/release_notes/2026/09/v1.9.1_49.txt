v1.9.1 (Build 49)

Security
- Approving a passwordless sign-in now means typing the 4-digit code shown on the other device. The old way put three codes on screen and let you tap one, which is a one-in-three guess for anyone holding your primary phone, and nothing counted the tries. That way is gone.
- BeatzLock no longer sends the code, or any list containing it, to the approving phone. The only place it appears is the screen of the device asking to sign in.
- Wrong codes are limited to five. After that the request retires itself and the other device has to ask again.

Fixes and Performance
- Your synced vault is never replaced by what is on one device. Settings has a new "Repair Cloud Sync Key" for when your devices cannot open it: on a device that can still read it, this re-locks it with the key your email and password produce now, so your other devices can open it again after entering their password once. It reads what is stored before writing, so anything in the cloud that is not on that device is kept.
- If no device can open your synced vault, BeatzLock says so and changes nothing. It will not overwrite it, because that stored copy is the only one there is and a key may still exist on a device you have not opened in a while.
- Changing your password no longer loses synced data. It used to upload only the accounts on the device you changed it from, straight over the stored copy, and it left synced passwords and secure texts locked under the old password for good. It now re-locks all three, and reads what is stored before writing it.
- Your accounts on the device are never touched by any of this. They are stored under a separate key that your password does not affect.
