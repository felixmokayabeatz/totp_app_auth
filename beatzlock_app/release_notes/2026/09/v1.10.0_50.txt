v1.10.0 (Build 50)

What's New
- Your recovery codes PDF can now unlock your synced accounts. Until now those codes only got you back into your account; they could not open your synced vault. When you generate recovery codes, BeatzLock locks a copy of your vault key with them, so the PDF is a real way back. The key itself never leaves your device, and our servers never see it.
- Settings has "Unlock Sync With A Recovery Code" for when no device can open your synced vault. Enter one code from your PDF and that device can read your accounts again.
- Changing your password keeps recovery working. The new key is recorded against the old one, so codes you saved before the change still lead to it. You do not need to generate new codes every time.

Security
- Resetting your password now warns you first. Your synced accounts are locked with a key made from your current password, and a reset happens without that password, so it cannot unlock them. Before, this happened silently and the synced copy could not be opened again. The reset screen now says so plainly, and reminds you that accounts already on a signed-in device stay on that device.
- Generate a fresh set of recovery codes once you are on this version. Codes from before it have no vault key attached to them.

Fixes and Performance
- A wrong recovery code is refused outright rather than producing a wrong key, so a mistyped code can never be mistaken for a real one.
