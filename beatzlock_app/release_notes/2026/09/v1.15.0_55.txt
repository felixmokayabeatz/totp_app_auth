v1.15.0 (Build 55)

What's New
- Import now reads both of BeatzLock's own export files, alongside the formats it already handled. An encrypted backup asks for its passphrase; a plain otpauth file just imports.
- Import works on Linux and Fedora, and on macOS. It was previously limited to Android and Windows, so a Linux user had no way to get accounts in at all.
- Accounts you already have are skipped rather than added twice, and BeatzLock names them: "Already in your vault: GitHub, AWS". A duplicate is now caught by its secret as well as its name, so the same account exported from another app under a different label is still recognised.
- Import and Export now sit together in Settings, and "Import OTP Accounts" is called "Import All Accounts".

Changed
- Exporting is done from your primary device. It writes every secret you have into one file, so it belongs on the device your account already treats as the main one. Importing still works from any device, which is the point: a device you have just set up is the one that needs it.
- Passwords and Secure Self Text have been removed. Both are gone from the app, and the data they synced is deleted from our servers. Your OTP accounts are unaffected.

Fixes and Performance
- Signing out now also stops push notifications, which previously kept arriving for an account that had already signed out.
- Restore points no longer mention passwords. They were still reporting "and 0 passwords" after the feature was removed.
- The notifications screen no longer offers a filter for secure self texts, and old notifications for the removed features are cleared out rather than left sitting in your list with nothing to open.
- Tightened security settings on our servers: admin sessions are now restricted to encrypted connections.
