v1.8.1 (Build 46)

What's New
- One way in, not two. If your phone or computer can unlock BeatzLock with its own PIN, pattern, fingerprint, face or Windows Hello, that is now the only way in. Cancelling the unlock takes you back to the lock screen instead of quietly offering a separate app PIN. The BeatzLock PIN is still there, but only on devices that have no screen lock of their own.
- BeatzLock now keeps up when you change your device security. Add a screen lock and it switches over and deletes the old BeatzLock PIN it no longer needs. Remove your screen lock and it asks you to set a fresh BeatzLock PIN straight away, rather than expecting you to remember an old one. Either way it tells you what changed, so the way in never moves without warning.
- A show/hide eye on every hidden box, so you can check what you typed before you send it. Passwords, PINs and unlock codes all have one now.
- Plain-English messages when BeatzLock cannot reach the server. "No internet connection" now says exactly that, instead of talking about DNS or certificates, and a sync that fails tells you why and gives you a Retry button.
- Every pop-up has a way out. Approval prompts, pickers, and the reminder to save your Recovery Codes PDF all have a Close, Cancel or Later button.
- A brighter, more polished look: twelve named accent colours to pick from, softer cards, rounder controls, tidier text and consistent pop-ups everywhere.

Fixes and Performance
- The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy now read the same in the app as on the website. Characters like the bullet separator, dashes, curly quotes and accents used to show up as raw codes such as "&middot;".
- Pop-ups with a text box no longer push their buttons off screen in landscape, so Confirm and Cancel stay reachable on tablets and sideways phones.
- On Windows and Linux the window can no longer be dragged smaller than it can actually draw, which used to leave striped bars across the header and the code cards. On a genuinely narrow screen the six-digit code now moves below the account name instead of being squeezed.
- The home screen no longer rebuilds and re-sorts your whole vault every second just to move the countdown. Large vaults scroll smoothly and use less battery.
- Faster start-up: storage and background services now start together instead of one after another.
- Fixed several pop-ups that held on to memory each time they were opened, a code timer that could run twice after retrying a vault load, and a progress window that could not be dismissed if the network stalled.
