v1.8.2 (Build 47)

Security
- Sign-in codes, password-reset codes and the delete-everything code now stop accepting guesses after five wrong tries. Request a fresh code and it arrives in your inbox, where only you can read it.
- Those codes are now produced by a stronger random source.

What's Changed
- Your email address is your account. Usernames are gone everywhere, in the app and on our servers. Nothing changes about how you sign in.
- Emails from BeatzLock now simply open with "Hello," and never use a name.

Fixes and Performance
- Codes stop ticking while BeatzLock is off screen, so it uses less battery in the background. Reopening the app refreshes them straight away.
- Faster start-up: services that do not depend on each other now start together.
- Your notifications list and the download pages on our website load noticeably quicker.
