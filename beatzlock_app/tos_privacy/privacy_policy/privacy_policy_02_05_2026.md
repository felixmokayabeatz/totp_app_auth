# BeatzLock Authenticator Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BeatzLock Authenticator collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares information when you use the BeatzLock app, account services, sync services, update services, recovery features, device session features, notification features, ratings, APIs, and related websites.

BeatzLock is designed for security and privacy. A key goal is to collect only what is needed to operate the app and to keep sensitive OTP data encrypted when sync is used.

## 2. Information you provide

Depending on how you use BeatzLock, you may provide:

- Username.
- Email address.
- Password.
- OTP account names, issuers, labels, and secrets that you add to the app.
- Backup and recovery code actions.
- Profile information, such as display details or profile photo if supported.
- App rating, comments, suggestions, or feedback.
- Support messages or other information you send directly.

Passwords are used for authentication and should be stored in protected form on the server. BeatzLock does not need your plain password after login.

## 3. Information created while using the service

BeatzLock may create or store service records such as:

- Account ID and account status.
- Login and authentication tokens.
- Device session records, including device labels and device references.
- Primary-device status.
- Sync status, sync timestamps, version counters, and deleted-account markers.
- Security events, such as sign-ins, password changes, PIN changes, backup code generation, account additions, and active-device checks.
- App notification records.
- App update and version request information.
- Server logs, error logs, IP addresses, timestamps, and request metadata needed to operate and protect the service.

## 4. Encrypted OTP and backup data

When cloud sync is enabled, BeatzLock stores synced OTP account data as encrypted content. This may include OTP account names, issuers, usernames, secrets, and related account details, but the sync payload is intended to be encrypted before storage and transmission.

Backup code sets and notification payloads may also be stored in encrypted form where supported by the service.

Because encrypted data depends on the correct key or password-derived key, BeatzLock may not be able to recover your OTP accounts if you lose the information needed to decrypt them.

## 5. Local device storage

The BeatzLock app may store information locally on your device, including:

- OTP accounts.
- App settings.
- Theme and preference settings.
- Login tokens.
- Encryption key material.
- App lock or PIN-related data.
- Sync preferences.

Local data may remain on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, clear app data, sign out, or use a deletion feature in the app.

Your device operating system, backups, malware protection, cloud device backups, and physical access to your device may affect the privacy and security of local data.

## 6. How BeatzLock uses information

BeatzLock uses information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Sign you in and keep sessions secure.
- Sync encrypted OTP data across your devices if you enable sync.
- Restore access with backup or recovery features where available.
- Show security notifications.
- Manage active devices and primary-device controls.
- Provide app updates and version checks.
- Display legal documents and app information.
- Process ratings, feedback, and support requests.
- Detect abuse, investigate errors, protect the service, and keep records needed for security.
- Improve app reliability, usability, and security.
