v1.7.0 (Build 37)

What's New
- Added Server Lockdown in Settings. After PIN confirmation, it fully blocks BeatzLock from talking to the internet or server until you turn it off.
- Server Lockdown now shuts down sign-in, passwordless, sync, update checks, ratings, legal pages, and other online features while keeping the local vault available offline.
- Individual local OTP-account deletion now requires your BeatzLock PIN.
- Individual Delete Account Everywhere now requires the exact phrase "Delete Account Everywhere", your BeatzLock PIN, and the current primary device.
- Passwordless sign-in codes now use 4 digits and rotate every 60 seconds for a smoother approval window.
