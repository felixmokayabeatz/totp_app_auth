v1.6.0 (Build 15)

What's new
- Added offline-first use, so you can use the authenticator without creating an account.
- Signing in still enables automatic backup and cross-platform sync.
- Local accounts now stay available on the same device after signing out.
- Added Google Authenticator imports, including transfer QR codes with multiple accounts.
- QR export now shows the human-friendly Base32 secret by default, with advanced Base64 hidden behind a reveal.
- Remote sign-out now reacts faster through the heartbeat flow.
- Accent colors now apply more consistently across the app with refreshed UI touches.
- Reviews now require a signed-in account.
- Backup PDF download now uses the device/app unlock flow before exporting.
- Improved offline account deletion messaging and sync sign-in return flow.
